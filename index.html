// about

Rigor over headlines.

Ricky Gole is a machine learning researcher completing an M.S. in Advanced Computing at Morgan State University (4.0 GPA, graduating December 2026). His research centers on the empirical evaluation of machine learning systems: finding the failure modes that stay hidden in benchmark scores and only surface once a model is deployed.

His findings include clinical LLM ensembles that converge on the same wrong answer 19.9× more often than chance, drug-interaction models that collapse from 0.73 to 0.00 F1 on post-2019 FDA data, and a directional scoring bias against African American English in a fine-tuned essay scorer. Where the evidence supports one, he builds the fix: a safety routing rule with 96.1% precision, a retrieval probe that partially recovers the collapsed drug model, and a fusion model that holds steady when a noisy data layer is added.

He builds the control arm that decides whether a result is real: a seven-arm ablation designed to falsify his own hypothesis, a reproducibility release that recomputes every headline number from raw predictions, and multi-agent systems that verify their own citations. A background in SOC monitoring and infrastructure operations keeps his work focused on systems that hold up in production.

Selected Research

Every project starts from one question: is this result actually real? The key finding is up top.

Apr – Jun 2026IEEE ICDM 2026

Convergent Failure Auditing of Clinical LLM Ensembles

19.9×unanimous wrong answers vs. chance (24.6% vs. 1.23%)
  • Audited Llama-3-8B, Qwen-2.5-7B, Gemma-3n, GPT-4o, and DeepSeek-V3 on 4,089 MedQA and MedMCQA questions.
  • A distractor-shuffle control showed the failure is content-driven, not a letter-position artifact.
  • Built a parameter-free safety router with 96.1% precision at 6.7–8.0% human-review cost.
Mar 2025 – Apr 2026Clinical NLP

Temporal OOD Audit of Clinical NLP (KnowledgeRAG-DDI)

0.73 → 0.00 F1BiomedBERT collapse on post-2019 FDA drug pairs
  • Built a 600-pair out-of-distribution set; the collapse reproduced across four seeds.
  • A name-only control traced it to structural dependence on sentence context, not drug novelty.
  • Recovered 0.264 F1 with a hybrid BM25 + FAISS retrieval probe over FDA labels.
Mar – Jun 2026Fairness

DART: Dialect Robustness Benchmarking for Automated Essay Scoring

β = −0.029significant negative scoring drift for African American English in a fine-tuned scorer (p < 0.001)
  • 480 anchor-variant essay pairs across six U.S. English dialect families, with a 92.71% validation pass rate.
  • Showed chain-of-thought prompting amplifies drift across GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, and Llama-3.2-3B judges.
May – Jun 2026Multi-omics

Signal-Gated Omic Fusion (SGOF)

0.664 AUROC heldwith a noise layer present, while MOGONET dropped 0.149 (best single modality: 0.692)
  • Showed an uninformative 258,782-SNP layer drags four fusion families below the best single modality.
  • Designed a graph-convolutional fusion that weights modalities by held-out validation signal.

Provenance-Conditioned Clinical Encoder: A Falsification Study

  • An arbitrary segment-index bias (3.408 val loss) beat true authorship labels (3.531).
  • Showed pretraining loss doesn't predict downstream mortality AUROC across 36,504 examples.
