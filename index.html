About

I find where AI systems fail, and fix them.

Ricky Gole is a machine learning researcher finishing an M.S. in Advanced Computing at Morgan State University (4.0 GPA, graduating December 2026). He stress-tests LLMs, clinical NLP, and retrieval systems to find the failures that benchmark scores hide, then builds safeguards where the evidence supports them.

In 2026 he had 3 first-author papers accepted at IEEE ICDM, EDM, and IEEE Cloud Summit, plus co-authored work at ACM Learning@Scale and IEEE BIBM 2025. His findings include clinical LLM ensembles that pick the same wrong answer 19.9× more often than chance, and a drug-interaction model that collapses from 0.73 to 0.00 F1 on newer FDA data. His fixes include a safety routing rule with 96.1% precision. Before graduate school, he worked in SOC monitoring and IT infrastructure, which keeps his research grounded in systems that have to hold up in production.

Research

Selected Research

Provenance-Conditioned Clinical Encoder (Falsification Study) Aug 2026 to Sep 2026

  • Falsified a provenance-conditioning hypothesis in a from-scratch clinical transformer encoder: across seven architecture arms and three seeds, an arbitrary segment-index bias (3.4078 val_loss) beat true authorship labels (3.5310).
  • Showed pretraining loss does not predict downstream performance. On multi-note mortality prediction (36,504 examples), a randomly initialized model reached 0.8014 AUROC vs. 0.7998 for the pretrained block-mask model.
  • Built the full control set and found that a fixed block mask beats the learned bias it was meant to justify (3.2772 vs. 4.3554 val_loss for the vanilla baseline).

Multi-Omic Fusion Robustness: Signal-Gated Omic Fusion May 2026 to Jun 2026

  • Diagnosed modality dominance in antidepressant-response prediction: an uninformative 258,782-SNP genotype layer drags four fusion families below the best single modality (methylation, 0.692 AUROC).
  • A controlled synthetic-noise experiment showed naive fusion declining from 0.651 to 0.518 AUROC as dimensionality scales to 100,000, with strict within-fold feature selection.
  • Built SGOF, a graph-convolutional architecture that weights modalities by held-out signal. It holds AUROC at 0.664 with the noise layer present, while MOGONET dropped 0.149.

DART: Dialect Robustness Benchmarking for Automated Essay Scoring Mar 2026 to Jun 2026

  • Built a benchmark of 480 anchor-variant essay pairs across six U.S. English dialect families plus 160 controls, validated by a three-gate pipeline (length drift, DeBERTa-v3 NLI, feature presence) with a 92.71% pass rate.
  • Exposed a directional bias against African American English in a fine-tuned scorer: AAE was the only family with significant negative drift (β = -0.029, p < 0.001).
  • Showed chain-of-thought scaffolding amplifies scoring drift across GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, and Llama-3.2-3B judges.

Convergent Failure Auditing of Clinical LLM Ensembles Apr 2026 to Jun 2026 · IEEE ICDM 2026 · First author

  • Evaluated five models (Llama-3-8B, Qwen-2.5-7B, Gemma-3n, GPT-4o, DeepSeek-V3) on 4,089 MedQA and MedMCQA questions. Models pick the same wrong answer far more than independence predicts (24.6% unanimous-wrong vs. 1.23% chance, a 19.9x gap).
  • A distractor-letter shuffle showed the failure is content-driven, not a letter-position artifact.
  • Built a parameter-free safety routing rule reaching 96.1% precision on MedQA at 6.7 to 8.0% human-review cost.

Strategic Friction Mining in Multi-Turn LLM Collaboration Mar 2026 to Jun 2026

  • Built a controlled two-policy simulation pipeline contrasting a seamless-answer agent with a Strategic Friction Framework across a 600-task benchmark spanning three domains and three personas.
  • Encoded interaction traces into an 11-state symbolic vocabulary and mined them with PrefixSpan, transition-graph entropy, and UMAP + HDBSCAN clustering.

Temporal OOD Audit of Clinical NLP (KnowledgeRAG-DDI) Mar 2025 to Apr 2026

  • On a 600-pair post-2019 FDA out-of-distribution set, BiomedBERT drug-interaction models collapsed from 0.7273 to 0.0000 F1 across four seeds.
  • A controlled name-only check traced the collapse to structural dependence on sentence context rather than drug novelty.
  • Recovered 0.2640 F1 with KnowledgeRAG-DDI, a hybrid BM25 + FAISS retrieval probe over FDA labels.

Benchmark Validity Audit of Knowledge Tracing Mar 2026 to Apr 2026

  • Across 95.3 million EdNet interactions, a 2-feature baseline recovers 35.7% of Deep Knowledge Tracing's above-chance AUC.
  • Introduced the recovery ratio as a reusable benchmark diagnostic, with calibration (Brier, ECE) and multi-seed stability checks.

Prescriptive Persistence: LLM Evaluation and Human-AI Alignment Dec 2025 to Jan 2026 · ACM L@S 2026 · Co-author

  • Co-developed the Prescriptive Gap metric across 12,979 ELL sentences, finding a 9.1% mean over-intervention rate vs. expert benchmarks (Cohen's d = 0.91).
  • A frugal-prompt intervention cut GPT-5.2 over-correction from 42.9% to 6.6% on Korean L1 sentences.

The Semantic Gap in Behavioral Embeddings for Educational RAG Dec 2025 to Jan 2026 · EDM 2026 · First author

  • Built a 50-dimensional SVD behavioral manifold from 15.8 million interaction records and found near-zero correlation with semantic embeddings (r = 0.006).
  • Reduced hallucination (p < 0.001) by fine-tuning Llama-3-8B with DPO and LoRA on 600 contrastive pairs, validated on 122 prompts with 10,000 bootstrap resamples.

BIO-DQNA: Safety-Constrained Reinforcement Learning May 2025 to Aug 2025 · IEEE BIBM 2025 · Co-author

  • Raised policy success to 94% while cutting the unsafe-action rate from 12% to 2% with clinically tiered rewards in a custom PyTorch environment.
  • Benchmarked against six DQN variants (through Rainbow) with 100 independent seeds per configuration.

Cloud Security: AWS Confused Deputy Mitigation Jan 2025 to May 2025 · IEEE Cloud Summit 2026 · First author

  • Blocked cross-account confused deputy attacks across six controlled scenarios using two isolated AWS accounts and ExternalId enforcement.
  • Processed 1 million CloudTrail events with entropy-based anomaly detection, and derived closed-form bounds on attacker success as a function of ExternalId entropy.
